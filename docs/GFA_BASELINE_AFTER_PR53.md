## Implemented Foundational Layers

### Layer -2: RealityGeometry ✅
**Package**: `src/gfa/reality/`

**Core Principle**: No thinking without reality; reality is the precondition for the system's possibility, not generated by it.

**Key Components**:
- `RealityDomain` (Self, Life, World, Relational)
- `RealityStatus` (Physical → Biological → Social → Conceptual → Symbolic → Hypothetical)
- `MinimalRealityUnit`
- `RealityEffect` (what reality produces, not reality itself)
- `EmbodiedObserver` (observer is part of reality)
- `RealityBridge` (mode transfer validation)
- `RealityValidator`

**Critical Laws**:
- Reality is NOT produced by the system
- Reality CANNOT be CERTIFIED by the system (reality precedes certification)
- Hypothetical reality CANNOT produce physical effects without bridge
- No mode transfer without bridge validation

### Layer -1: SensoryTransferGeometry ✅
**Package**: `src/gfa/sensory_transfer/`

**Core Principle**: Reality does NOT enter the algebra directly; all traces come through channel-bound sensory or instrumental transfer.

**Key Components**:
- `SensoryChannel` (with ChannelType)
- `Observer` and `Instrument` (with CalibrationState)
- `Signal` and `NoiseProfile`
- `SensoryTrace`
- `SensoryTransferEvent`
- `SensoryTransferValidator`

**Critical Laws**:
- No sensory transfer without channel
- No sensory transfer without time/place/reference
- No sensory transfer without noise/residual accounting
- Sensory trace ≠ External reality
- Visual/auditory signals do NOT certify sources
- SensoryTransfer may produce FirstPriorUnit only as CANDIDATE or lower
- No CERTIFIED rank from sensory transfer alone

### Layer 0: ProtoPrior Kernel ✅
**Package**: `src/gfa/proto_prior/`

**Core Principle**: FirstPriorUnit is the minimal sufficient unit for entering Prior Geometry.

**Key Components**:
- `FirstPriorUnit` (14 mandatory fields + auto-generated trace_id)
- `ExistenceType`, `Domain`, `Channel`
- `TimeAnchor`, `PlaceAnchor`, `ReferenceAnchor`
- `Distinction`, `Boundary`
- `RetentionState`, `ComparabilityState`, `PrimitiveBindability`
- `Rank`, `Residual`
- `ProtoPriorValidator`

**14 Mandatory Fields**:
1. `entity_or_effect`
2. `existence_type`
3. `domain`
4. `distinction`
5. `boundary`
6. `time_anchor`
7. `place_anchor`
8. `reference_anchor`
9. `channel`
10. `retention_state`
11. `comparability_state`
12. `primitive_bindability`
13. `residuals`
14. `rank`

**Critical Laws**:
- FirstPriorUnit CANNOT produce rule
- FirstPriorUnit CANNOT produce meaning
- FirstPriorUnit CANNOT issue judgment
- FirstPriorUnit CANNOT certify
- Hypothetical existence CANNOT be CERTIFIED
- Textual/symbolic/acoustic existence does NOT imply physical existence without bridge
- Residuals must be preserved
- Trace ID must be stable and unique

### Layer 0.5: CognitiveCarrierGeometry ✅
**Package**: `src/gfa/cognitive_carrier/`

**Core Principle**: No cognitive processing without an admissible CognitiveCarrier with declared minimal capacities.

**Key Components**:
- `CognitiveCarrier` (with 10 mandatory capacity fields)
- `EmbodiedState` (with EmbodimentType)
- `CarrierCapacity` (with CapacityLevel)
- 8 specific capacity types:
  - `SensoryCapacity`
  - `AttentionCapacity`
  - `MemoryCapacity`
  - `ComparisonCapacity`
  - `BindingCapacity`
  - `InterpretationCapacity`
  - `FeedbackCapacity`
  - `OutputCapacity`
- `CarrierValidator`

**Critical Laws** (37 tests enforcing these):
- Carrier does NOT create meaning
- Carrier does NOT issue judgment
- Carrier does NOT certify claims
- Carrier does NOT raise epistemic rank
- Carrier capacity is NOT proof of correctness
- Attention affects priority, NOT rank
- Memory recall ≠ original trace
- Primitive binding does NOT raise rank
- Interpretation does NOT create meaning
- Missing capacity must become residual or failure
